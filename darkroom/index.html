Finding beauty in the ordinary

I’m an artist with a camera, based in East London. I shoot portraiture, street scenes and urban landscapes.

I started out documenting school and college life, then sport: moments of grace, heartbreak and pure joy, frozen in time. Now my work lives in the moody, dark and emotional moments of real life, and the little sparks of wonderment inside them.

I look for the perfect collision of light, mood, shadow, space, emotion and story.

Go a little bit out of your depth. And when you don’t feel that your feet are quite touching the bottom, you’re just about in the right place to do something exciting.

David Bowie, a lifelong hero of mine
