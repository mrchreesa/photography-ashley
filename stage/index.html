Finding beauty in the ordinary

I’m an artist with a camera, based in East London. I shoot portraiture, street scenes, urban landscapes and the stage.

I started out documenting school and college life, then sport: moments of grace, heartbreak and pure joy, frozen in time. Now my work lives in the moody, dark and emotional moments of real life, and the little sparks of wonderment inside them.

David Bowie has been a hero of mine for as long as I can remember. He taught me to stay inquisitive and look at the world differently. That’s how I take my photographs.

Go a little bit out of your depth.
